# CyberSkinOS 1.0.0

The first release of CyberSkinOS, the holographic space-station home screen from Stellarite Corporation.

## The look
- One style, built in full: panels of projected glass with cut corners, corner brackets and scan lines, over a star
  field with a slow orbit ring.
- The Stellarite star is the home button: tap for the Databank, double-tap for the Console, hold and drag to move it.
- Eight palettes (Stellarite, Cobalt, Nebula, Pulsar, Solar, Ice, Biolume, Graphite), eight signal colours, and
  sliders to mix your own.
- Projected clock digits, dials of light, segmented Vitals bars, a holographic data disc for music, and a hexagon
  floodlight.
- Gentle motion: panels shimmer now and then, a scan line sweeps on page change, and pulse rings spread from the star.
- Soft glassy sound effects, off until you switch them on.

## Widgets
Holo clock, Time readout, Chrono dial, Time display, Power cell, Data storage, Vitals, Audio deck, System load,
Nav bearing, Atmos pressure, Lunar phase, Daylight cycle, Atmos (weather), Orbital map, Stopwatch, Timer,
Calendar grid, Schedule feed, Floodlight, World time, Comms, Usage log, Log, Quick access, Data folder, Link cache,
Priority apps, and External widgets from your other apps.

## Everything else
Up to eight home pages, presets you can save and share as a text code, scheduled looks, hidden apps, a Recent row,
one-file backup and restore, and safe mode.

## Install
Download `CyberSkinOS-v1.0.0.apk` below, open it on your phone, then press Home and choose CyberSkinOS.
It installs alongside BrassWorks and any other launcher.
